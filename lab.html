About Python Jupyter Lab

The Python button opens Lang & T's browser-based Jupyter Lab. Use it for notebooks, Python files, data analysis, visualisations, and guided AI exercises. Your work normally stays in your browser unless you download it or save it elsewhere.

How to use the lab

  1. Open the practical lab that matches your course.
  2. Follow your lesson instructions and complete the exercise.
  3. Test your work, fix errors, and document what you learned.
  4. Download or save completed work to your GitHub portfolio when instructed.
  5. Ask your Lang & T mentor for support when you are stuck.
